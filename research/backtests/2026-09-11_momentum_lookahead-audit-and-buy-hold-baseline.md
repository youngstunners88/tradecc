# Look-Ahead Audit and Buy-and-Hold Baseline — momentum, SOL/USDC

**Date:** 2026-09-11
**Trigger:** `planning/architecture/tradingagents-reference-notes.md`
(TradingAgents fixed look-ahead/point-in-time bugs in their own harness)
and `research/backtest-methodology-references.md` (freqtrade/jesse
maturity checks).
**Scope:** audit `research/walk_forward.py` and `src/backtest/runner.py`
for look-ahead, and confirm the baseline comparison our own protocol
requires is actually being reported.

## Headline: the strategy loses to buy-and-hold on every interval

`backtest.md` and the `backtesting` skill have required a buy-and-hold
comparison from the start. **It had never been computed.** It is the
single most informative number the project has produced:

| Window | Strategy net | **Buy-and-hold net** |
|---|---|---|
| 1h — 2026-07-29 → 09-09 | +$0.58 | **+$3.93** |
| 4h — 2026-03-27 → 09-09 | −$0.55 | **+$1.70** |
| 15m — 2026-08-30 → 09-09 | −$1.78 | **−$0.28** |

Both figures are net of the same corrected cost model, $10 position,
buy-and-hold charged one full round trip including account rent.

**Buy-and-hold wins everywhere.** On 1h the strategy's best-ever
number — which had already failed replication — is about 15% of simply
holding SOL over the same window. On 15m the strategy loses **6×** more
than holding. There is no window in the data where trading this strategy
beat not trading it.

This does not depend on any of the defects below. It is a straight
comparison at the same costs, and it is the reason the "stop tuning
EMA/RSI momentum" recommendation is now unambiguous rather than merely
well-supported.

## Look-ahead audit: none found

The bug class TradingAgents hit is **not present**. Verified empirically,
not by reading alone:

- **Indicator warmup is strictly causal.** EMA, SMA and RSI were checked
  for prefix-invariance at three cut points each: `out[i]` is identical
  whether or not later candles exist. The recurrences only ever read
  backwards.
- **Strategy signals are prefix-invariant** at three cut points — a
  signal at bar *N* does not change when bars after *N* are appended.
- **Phase B has no leakage.** Parameters are selected on `candles[:lo]`;
  the test window is scored from a run over `candles[:hi]` keeping only
  entries in `[lo, hi)`. No data after the decision point is consulted.
- **Warmup does not leak across fold boundaries** — the specific edge
  case the freqtrade notes flag. Each test fold warms up from the bars
  *preceding* it, which are training data. That is the correct direction.
- A trade-level causality test already existed
  (`test_no_look_ahead_a_prefix_run_matches_the_truncated_full_run`),
  though it is a single 12-candle smoke test rather than a guarantee.

## Three real defects, all now fixed

None of them were look-ahead. All three were point-in-time realism
defects in the same family.

### 1. Decision timestamps were backdated by one bar interval

`Candle.timestamp` is the bar's **open** time, but every decision used
that bar's **close**. Signals, position open/close times, and the daily
circuit breaker's UTC-day bucketing were all stamped up to one full
interval before the triggering information existed.

- **P&L impact: none.** Prices were close-to-close and self-consistent.
- **Result impact: none.** The circuit breaker never fired in any run
  (0 blocks across all three intervals), so day mis-bucketing could not
  reach any reported number.
- It did diverge from paper mode, which stamps decisions at wall-clock.

Fixed: decisions are stamped at `candle.timestamp + interval`.

### 2. Stops were evaluated on the close only; high/low were never read

`Candle` carries `high` and `low`; the runner, stop evaluation and paper
trader never read either. A stop hit mid-bar that recovered by the close
was invisible — a loss the position really took, silently skipped.

Measured before fixing: **1h 0 trades affected, 15m 0 affected, 4h 1
trade.** Fixed: the risk engine is now asked about the bar's low and
high, not just its close, and the exit is marked at the threshold level.
Stop wins when one bar spans both thresholds, since OHLC cannot say which
came first.

### 3. Paper mode acted on the still-forming bar

`fetch_candles` output was used whole. The provider returns the current,
in-progress bar alongside finished ones, and its "close" is just the live
price — it changes on every poll. Backtests only ever see completed bars,
so paper evidence was being generated by a **different process** than the
backtest that validated the strategy. This was the most consequential of
the three, because the 30-day gate rests on paper evidence.

Fixed: `core/candles.completed_candles` drops any bar that has not closed
by the decision time; paper mode applies it before generating a signal.

### Hardening found while fixing

The runner previously derived bar duration from `config.data.candle_interval`.
A caller replaying a 1h series under a 15m config would stamp decisions on
boundaries no bar has. The runner now takes bar duration **from the candle
series itself** — the data is the authority on how long its own bars are.

## Effect on Phase A / Phase B: conclusions unchanged

Re-ran Phase A at default parameters with all three fixes in place:

| Interval | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1h | +0.5815 | **+0.5815** | none — bit-identical |
| 4h | −0.4264 | **−0.5494** | −0.1230 (the intrabar stop) |
| 15m | −1.7785 | **−1.7785** | none — bit-identical |

Per-fold results are unchanged for 1h and 15m. 4h moves slightly more
negative. **All three intervals still fail the locked replication rule**,
for the same reasons as before: 1h on concentration, 4h on a negative
pool, 15m on everything.

So the defects were real, worth fixing, and did not flatter any reported
result. The one measurable change runs *against* the strategy.
